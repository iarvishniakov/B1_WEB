B1_WEB A1 fallback refactor
===========================

New files
---------
market_data_service.py
pages_app/settings.py

Persistent setting
------------------
The app creates public.b1_settings automatically (if DB role permits DDL) and stores:
  overwrite_ib_with_a1 = true/false

IB fallback rule
----------------
Raw public.md_snap is never modified.
IB is considered unusable for fallback when:
- bid/ask missing
- bid/ask <= 0
- bid > ask
- delayed = 'd'
- snapshot_at missing/invalid
- snapshot_at older than 90 seconds

When override is ON and matching a1_md_all MTM is available and positive:
- effective bid = MTM
- effective ask = MTM
- delayed = 'a1'
- effective_source = 'A1'

A1 aliases IBKR:* and IB:* are normalized to IB:*.
Trade status remains the IB trade status; A1 fixes market data, not whether the exchange is open.

Refactored consumers
--------------------
- Models
- Spreads
- Curves (live price loader)
- Russian SSF Rates
- Market Data
- Price Mapping status lookup

Pages using only MOEX data (Index Arb, Open Interest, RFLB Curve) were intentionally left unchanged.

Settings page
-------------
Sidebar -> Settings -> Market Data Control
Shows:
- IB delayed/stalled
- Replaced from A1
- Still invalid
- expandable instrument diagnostics

Important
---------
b1_price_mapping remains a database-derived mapping table. Its IB source status display now uses the
effective IB/A1 feed. The mapping table's own bid/ask values are not synthetically recomputed in Python,
because its database mapping formula should remain the source of truth.
